// G. Writing: office or remote?

A small company with 12 employees is moving to a new building. The boss asks you: should everybody work in the office, should people work remotely, or should the company do both? Write 5 to 7 sentences. Give your advice and your reasons. Use at least two verbs with to, one sentence with I recommend + -ing, one sentence with It depends on whether and one sentence with There is / There are. Write it yourself, without a translator. Julia reads it and gives feedback.
